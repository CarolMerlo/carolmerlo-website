There Are Two Ways to Work With Me

Both begin with the same question: what is the pattern that keeps stopping you — and what would your life and business look like if that pattern were gone?

I work with conscious entrepreneurs and spiritual seekers who are ready to close the gap between where they are and what they know is possible for them. Sometimes that work enters through the business. Sometimes it enters through something more personal. Either way, we almost always end up working on the same thing — the inner conditions that determine what your outer life can hold.

The two paths below will help you find the right starting point. If you're not sure which fits, the discovery call is the place to start — it's a real conversation, not a sales pitch, and it will become clear.
